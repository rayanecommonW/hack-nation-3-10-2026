Compounds per hour. agentic lab 65,853.7 vs serial manual 50.7. At this rate the lab pre-registers 105,366 hypotheses per 24h vs 4 in one human day.
The speedup is dominated by descriptor computation and by parallelism, not by model reasoning. On a 60-compound panel a single human analyst takes minutes, not hours, so the absolute ratio flatters the agentic lab at this size. The honest claim is the compounds-per-hour scaling, which is the quantity that matters at 10^4-10^6 compounds.
