01

Which approved drugs shut down the signal that lets Pseudomonas coordinate an infection?

PQS is the molecule this bacterium secretes to talk to itself. Blocking the conversation instead of the growth machinery is worth a look, because an anti-virulence drug is not expected to create the same resistance pressure as an antibiotic does.

That last sentence is a hypothesis, not a fact, and this lab treats it as one. It is also why the work below bothers with controls at all.

07

What this run does not show

  • 01No binding measurement. No docking, no affinity, no IC50. Direct signal quenching is unmeasured, and it is the thing that would actually matter.
  • 02Controls stayed out of the ranking. The eight positive and six negative controls were scored after ranking was frozen. None of them is a feature, a threshold or a weight.
  • 03Eight positives is a small sample. Read the denominators rather than the ratios.
  • 04One term in the accepted score is a literature prior. The ablation in the raw results says how much of the signal that single term carries.
  • 05The panel is sixty compounds, an embedded offline subset, not the full approved space of roughly sixteen hundred.
  • 06The throughput figure is descriptor arithmetic. It comes from computation and parallelism, not from model reasoning, and on a panel this small a single analyst would finish in an afternoon.
  • 07The hypotheses are this lab's own. The weights, gates and control sets were generated, not taken from a published model.

The next experiment is a LasR luciferase quench assay, run at sub-MIC against PAO1 and an isogenic lasI mutant. It needs a wet bench, so it sits behind human approval and no agent here is permitted to pretend otherwise.
